# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-users" %}Openings

There are currently no openings for PhD or postdoc positions.

If you are a talented early career researcher, have recently completed their PhD and are interested in joining the group for a postdoc, please reach out with a CV and a description of your research interests in relation to the group's research focus to explore grant opportunities (e.g., [NWO Veni](https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/nwo-talent-programme-veni-science-2026) or [MSCA](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships)).

If you are a student at the University of Amsterdam and interested in doing a Bachelor or Master project on marine ecosystem dynamics and biogeochemical cycling, which involves modeling and/or the analysis of (global) datasets, [contact the group leader](https://cara-nissen.github.io/Nissen-lab-website/contact/) at any time. The group will offer multiple projects each year.
